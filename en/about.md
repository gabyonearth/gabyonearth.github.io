Hi, I'm Gaby. I'm a geophysicist with a Master's in Geophysics from the Universidad de Chile and a researcher at its Seismic Risk Program (PRS). I chair the NGO CEUS Chile and I'm about to start a PhD in Earth Observation at Sapienza, in Rome.

I'm interested in how the Earth deforms, shakes and looks from space: GNSS, satellites, earthquakes, and how that science turns into risk management, memory and public policy. I represented Chilean youth at COP27 and I write opinion columns in Chilean media (you can find them under [Press]({{ '/en/columns/' | relative_url }})).

This blog is my field notebook: a place for photos, travels, food, conferences, conversations and the things I discover about satellites, geophysics and life as a scientist far from home.

I'm not trying to be famous. I just want to share what I live and learn.
