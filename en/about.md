Hi, I'm Gaby. I'm a geophysicist (Universidad de Chile) and I'm about to start a PhD in Earth Observation at Sapienza, in Rome.

I'm interested in how the Earth deforms, shakes and looks from space: GNSS, InSAR, satellites, earthquakes, and how that science turns into risk management, memory and public policy.

This blog is something like my travel journal during my PhD: a place to keep photos, trips, food, conferences, conversations and the things I discover about geophysics, what we can learn by looking at the Earth from above, and life far from home.

I intend to share reflections on what I live and learn.
