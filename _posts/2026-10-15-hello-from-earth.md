I'm starting this blog with a simple idea: to have a place to leave a trace of what I'm living. Maybe it will be useful to someone going through similar things (or maybe not). It comes from the idea of not romanticizing or martyring myself in this new stage of my life.

I'll write about **travel**, **science and satellites**, my **PhD in Rome**, **food**, being a **woman in science**, and personal reflections. I might interview people I meet along the way, and from time to time I'll link opinion columns I publish in Chilean media.

I wanted to step away a little from the social media space: no pressure, and nothing too dizzying. Instead, I want to have the time to reflect and decide what I do or don't want to share publicly. I want it to feel like writing my stories and thoughts in a travel notebook.
